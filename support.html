Help & Support Center

Learn how to sync, format, and secure your clipboard clipsets easily.

Instant Synchronization

Type or paste content on one device, and it synchronizes instantly across all connected screens. Access your clips by entering your custom identifier (ID) in the top nav.

Rich Markdown Editor

Format your clips using headings, bold, italic, code-highlighting, lists, and links. Use the newly added toolbar buttons to format tables, custom font families, or text colors.

Passcode Security

Need privacy? Tap "Set Password" inline next to your clipboard link. Unauthorized users visiting your link must supply the correct passcode to decrypt and edit the clipboard content.

Self-Destruct Timers

Configure a countdown timer for sensitive assets (10 mins, 1 hour, or 24 hours). Once the countdown completes, the database wipes the clipboard cleanly for safety.

Frequently Asked Questions

Simply click the QR Code button in the bottom floating toolbar to scan a share code directly, or click Copy Link next to your clipboard URL to copy the direct shortlink. Opening that link on any device connects it to your sync pool.

Highlight the text you wish to format, then click either the palette icon (Text Color) or T-icon (Text Font) in the editor's shortcuts bar. Pick your preset, and it wraps your text in HTML styling syntax (e.g. <span style="color: #ef4444">text</span>) which compiles on the Preview pane.

Split view splits your editor viewport into two side-by-side columns: the raw Markdown input panel on the left and the real-time compiled preview on the right. You can write content and see styling, tables, code blocks, lists, colors, and fonts render live.

When a passcode is assigned, ClipChain restricts viewport rendering. Visitors will only see a lock overlay screen. The actual data is synced dynamically only when the validated passcode is supplied.
